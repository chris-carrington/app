## What is a Whistleblower Policy?
<!--{"accordionBody":true}-->
-  A Whistleblower Policy encourages staff, volunteers, and Board members to report suspected fraud, violations of law, or other improper conduct without fear of retaliation
- Establishes a clear process for reporting and investigating concerns
- Signals that we welcome concerns an take them seriously
- Is our commitment to protecting those who speak up
<!--{"accordionEnd":true}-->


<!--{"accordionStart":true}-->
## What is a fraud?
<!--{"accordionBody":true}-->
- Fraud is when someone intentionally deceives others to gain money, property, or an unfair advantag
- It involves deliberate dishonesty, not just an honest mistake
- Examples:
    - An employee stealing donated funds by writing fake checks to themselves
    - A volunteer submitting fake receipts for expenses that were never actually incurred
    - A Board member secretly steering contracts to a business they own.
<!--{"accordionEnd":true}-->


<!--{"accordionStart":true}-->
## What is a retaliation?
<!--{"accordionBody":true}-->
-  Retaliation is when an organization or its leaders punish someone for reporting misconduct or cooperating with an investigation. This punishment can take many forms and is meant to discourage others from speaking up. 
- Examples:
    - A supervisor demoting a staff member who reported a coworker's theft
    - A Board member spreading false rumors about a volunteer who raised concerns about financial mismanagement
    - Firing or harassing someone for filing a whistleblower report
