## What is a Board (Board of Directors)?
- The Board's primary responsibility is to ensure the Nonprofit is fulfilling its mission, operating legally and following all internal policies
- Approves the annual budget and ensures funds are used properly

---

## What are Officers?
- Officers are Board members who are appointed by the Board to carry out specific operational duties
- California law requires a Nonprofit to have at least three specific Officer positions:
    - President (or Chair)
    - Secretary
    - Treasurer
- Officers are Board members with specific management duties beyond their general Board member oversight responsibilities

---

## What are Bylaws?
- Bylaws are the internal rulebook for how a Nonprofit will be managed and operated
- Defines the Board structure, Officer roles, meeting procedures, and how decisions are made
- Adopted by the Board and does not need to be filed with the State
