## When is a contractor's license required?
<!--{"accordionBody":true}-->
- When the total job cost (labor & materials) is $1,000 or more
<!--{"accordionEnd":true}-->


<!--{"accordionStart":true}-->
## What is the contractor's license experience requirement?
<!--{"accordionBody":true}-->
- Four years of experience w/in the last 10 years as any of the following:
- Journeyperson
    - Can perform the trade w/o supervision
- Foreperson
    - Oversee journeypeople
- Supervising employee
    - Construction lead
- Three years of Apprenticeship/Educational/Technical training (still need 1 year of practical experience)
    - College Transcripts
    - Technical School Transcripts
    - Apprenticeship Completion Certificates
<!--{"accordionEnd":true}-->

## What is egress?
<!--{"accordionBody":true}-->
- An exit from an occupied portion of a building or structure to a public space
<!--{"accordionEnd":true}-->


<!--{"accordionStart":true}-->
## What is a perimeter line?
<!--{"accordionBody":true}-->
- A boundary (line) drawn on an architect's site plan
<!--{"accordionEnd":true}-->

## What is a fire barrier?
<!--{"accordionBody":true}-->
- Wall, floor, or ceiling assembly designed to slow down or stop the spread of fire and smoke
<!--{"accordionEnd":true}-->


<!--{"accordionStart":true}-->
## What does x hour fire barrier mean?
<!--{"accordionBody":true}-->
- Fire-resistance rating
- For x hours the barrier must:
    - Maintain structural integrity
    - Limit temperature rise on the unexposed side
    - Act as a barrier to flames and hot gases
