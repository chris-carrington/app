## What is a Board (Board of Directors)?
- The Board's primary responsibility is to ensure the Nonprofit is fulfilling its mission, operating legally and follows all internal policies
- Approves the annual budget and ensures funds are used properly

---

## What are Officers?
- Officers are individuals appointed by the Board to carry out specific operational duties
- California law requires a Nonprofit to have at least three specific Officer positions: a President (or Chair), a Treasurer and a Secretary
- Officers are also Board Directors who sit on the Board but their Officer titles give them specific management duties beyond their general oversight responsibilities as Board members

---

## What are Bylaws?
- The internal rulebook for how our Nonprofit will be managed and operated
- Defines the Board structure, Officer roles, meeting procedures, and how decisions are made
- Adopted by the Board and does not need to be filed with the state
