## What is a registered agent?
- A registered agent is a person or company officially designated to receive legal papers on behalf of a Nonprofit. Legal papers includes things like:
    - Lawsuits
    - Subpoenas
    - Official notices from the state or courts
- Our registered agent must have a physical street address in California and must be available at that address during normal business hours

---

## What is a dissolution clause?
- A dissolution clause says what happens to all the organization's assets (money, tools, property, etc.) if the Nonprofit ever dissolves (the legal process of closing down).
- Under California law, the assets cannot be given to Board members, employees, or anyone personally because all remaining assets must be distributed to another Nonprofit

---

## What is prohibition on private inurement?
- Prohibition is an order to stop
- Private inurement is using Nonprofit assets or earnings for the personal benefit of insiders
- To avoid private inurement, keep staff pay reasonable for the work being done, comparable to what others in similar roles earn and approved by the Board
- Nonprofits get special tax benefits because we exist to serve the public good, not private interests
- A private inurement prohibition ensures that Nonprofit funds stays mission focused

---

## What are Articles of Incorporation?
- The document we file with the state to legally bring our Nonprofit into existence
- Includes our organization's name, purpose, initial directors, and registered agent
- Also includes required IRS language about dissolution and the prohibition on private inurement
