## What is a registered agent?
- A registered agent is the person or company officially designated to receive legal papers on behalf of our Nonprofit. Legal papers includes things like:
    - Lawsuits
    - Subpoenas
    - Official notices from the state or courts
- The registered agent must have a physical street address in our state and must be available during normal business hours

---

## What is a dissolution clause?
- A dissolution clause says what happens to all the organization's assets (money, tools, property, etc.) if the Nonprofit ever dissolves (the legal process of closing down).
- Under California law, the assets cannot be given to you, board members, employees, or anyone else personally because all remaining assets must be distributed to another Nonprofit

---

## What is prohibition on private inurement?
- Private inurement means using Nonprofit assets or earnings for the personal benefit of insiders
- Staff pay must be reasonable for the work being done, comparable to what others in similar roles earn and approved by the board
- Nonprofits get special tax benefits because they exist to serve the public good, not private interests
- The private inurement prohibition ensures that the organization's money stays focused on its mission, not on enriching insiders

---

## What are Articles of Incorporation?
-  The document we file with the state to legally bring our Nonprofit into existence
- Includes our organization's name, purpose, initial directors, and registered agent
- Also includes required IRS language about dissolution and the prohibition on private inurement
